## Tier 1: Starter - $500/month

### Target Client Profile

Small local businesses that are just getting started with digital marketing or have been operating with little to no online presence. These are business owners who know they need to be found online but do not have the time, expertise, or budget for a full-scale marketing operation.

**Ideal Client Examples:**
- A solo plumber in Rockville who gets most work from word-of-mouth referrals but wants to appear in "plumber near me" searches
- A family-owned restaurant in Silver Spring that has a basic website but no search optimization
- An independent contractor (painter, handyman, electrician) looking to fill gaps in their schedule
- A new salon or barbershop wanting to build an initial online footprint

### Monthly Deliverables

#### 1. Google Business Profile (GBP) Optimization
- Complete profile setup or audit of existing profile
- Category optimization and attribute management
- Photo uploads and optimization (up to 5 photos/month)
- GBP post creation (2 posts/month) to keep the listing active and engaging
- Q&A monitoring and response management
- Hours, service area, and contact information verification

#### 2. Basic Local SEO (5 Keywords)
- Initial keyword research targeting 5 high-value local search terms
- On-page optimization for primary service pages
- Title tag and meta description optimization
- Local schema markup implementation (one-time during onboarding)
- Monthly keyword ranking tracking and adjustments
- Google Search Console monitoring

#### 3. Content Creation (4 Blog Posts/Month)
- 4 SEO-optimized blog posts (600-800 words each)
- AI-generated first drafts using advanced language models
- Human editing for accuracy, brand voice, and local relevance
- Internal linking strategy
- Local keyword integration (Rockville, Montgomery County, specific neighborhoods)
- Published directly to client's website (WordPress, Squarespace, or Wix)

#### 4. Monthly Performance Report
- Google Business Profile insights (views, searches, actions)
- Keyword ranking positions for all 5 tracked keywords
- Website traffic overview (organic search sessions)
- Actionable recommendations for the next month
- Delivered as a branded PDF via email on the 1st of each month

### Time Commitment
- **Weekly investment:** ~3 hours/week (12 hours/month)
- Breakdown: GBP management (0.5 hr), SEO monitoring/adjustments (0.5 hr), content creation and editing (1.5 hr), reporting and client communication (0.5 hr)

### Expected Results
- **Month 1-2:** Google Business Profile fully optimized and indexed; initial keyword baseline established
- **Month 3:** 20-30% increase in local search visibility; GBP listing appearing in more local searches
- **Month 6:** Consistent organic traffic growth; measurable increase in phone calls and direction requests from GBP
- **Month 12:** Established local search presence for all 5 target keywords; steady inbound lead flow

### What is NOT Included
- Social media management
- Paid advertising (PPC/Google Ads)
- Email marketing
- Citation building beyond initial GBP setup
- Review management or solicitation
- Website design or development
