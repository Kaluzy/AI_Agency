# Week 1 Quick-Start Action Plan

**AI-Enhanced Digital Marketing Agency**
**Location:** Rockville, Maryland & Greater Montgomery County

**Document Version:** 1.0

---

This document provides a detailed, hour-by-hour action plan for your first week of business operations. By Friday evening, you will have a legally registered business, a live online presence, professional service documentation, and your first outreach emails sent to prospective clients. Every task includes specific instructions, recommended tools, estimated costs, and expected completion times.

## Day 1: Business Foundation (Monday)

### Morning Session (9:00 AM - 11:00 AM)

- [ ] **Register LLC with Maryland SDAT ($100 online)**
  - Visit the Maryland State Department of Assessments and Taxation (SDAT) website at https://egov.maryland.gov/BusinessExpress
  - Select "Form a New Business Entity" and choose "Limited Liability Company (LLC)"
  - Recommended name format: "[Your Name] Digital Marketing LLC" or "[Brand Name] Marketing Solutions LLC"
  - Before filing, run a name availability search on the SDAT database to confirm your chosen name is not taken
  - You will need: business name, principal office address (your home address is acceptable), registered agent name and address (you can serve as your own registered agent in Maryland), and the name of at least one member/manager
  - Pay the $100 filing fee by credit card
  - Processing typically takes 7-10 business days for standard online filing; expedited processing is available for an additional fee ($50 for same-day)
  - Save your confirmation number and filing receipt immediately
  - **Note:** Maryland also requires an Annual Report filing each year (due by April 15) with a $300 fee. Set a calendar reminder now for April 2027.

- [ ] **Apply for EIN with IRS (free, instant online)**
  - Visit https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online
  - The online application is available Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM Eastern Time
  - Select "Limited Liability Company" as the entity type
  - Enter the number of members (1 for a single-member LLC)
  - For reason for applying, select "Started new business"
  - You will receive your EIN immediately upon completion -- save and print the confirmation letter (Form CP 575)
  - You will need this EIN for your business bank account, tax filings, and client contracts

- [ ] **Open business bank account (Mercury or Relay - no fees)**
  - **Mercury** (mercury.com) -- Recommended for digital-first businesses
    - No monthly fees, no minimum balance requirements
    - Free domestic wire transfers and ACH transfers
    - Built-in bookkeeping integrations
    - Virtual and physical debit cards
    - Apply online with your EIN, Articles of Organization (from SDAT), and government-issued ID
  - **Relay** (relayfi.com) -- Alternative option
    - No monthly fees, no minimum balance
    - Up to 20 individual checking accounts for organizing funds
    - Built-in profit-first accounting buckets
  - **Action:** Apply to one of these banks immediately after receiving your EIN. Approval typically takes 1-3 business days. In the meantime, you can begin setting up other tools.
  - **Tip:** Create a simple financial structure from day one. Set up at least two sub-accounts or categories: "Operating" (for revenue and expenses) and "Tax Reserve" (transfer 25-30% of all revenue here for quarterly estimated taxes).

- [ ] **Set up basic bookkeeping (Wave - free)**
  - Visit waveapps.com and create a free account
  - Wave provides free invoicing, accounting, and receipt scanning
  - Set up your Chart of Accounts with these categories:
    - **Income:** Service Revenue, Setup Fee Revenue
    - **Expenses:** Software Subscriptions, Advertising, Professional Development, Office Supplies, Contractor Payments, Business Insurance, Bank Fees
    - **Assets:** Business Checking Account, Accounts Receivable
    - **Liabilities:** Accounts Payable, Sales Tax Payable (if applicable)
  - Connect your business bank account once it is approved
  - Create your first invoice template with your business name, address, EIN, and payment terms (Net 15)

### Afternoon Session (1:00 PM - 3:00 PM)

- [ ] **Subscribe to essential tools**

  Complete the following subscriptions using your business email (set up in the next step) or personal email temporarily:

  | Tool | Purpose | Cost | Action |
  |------|---------|------|--------|
  | Claude Pro | AI content generation, strategy, analysis | $20/month | Subscribe at claude.ai |
  | Google Workspace | Business email, Drive, Docs, Calendar | $7/month | Subscribe at workspace.google.com |
  | Ubersuggest | SEO keyword research, site audits, rank tracking | $29/month | Subscribe at neilpatel.com/ubersuggest |
  | Buffer | Social media scheduling and analytics | $15/month | Subscribe at buffer.com (Essentials plan) |
  | Canva Pro | Graphic design for social posts, proposals, reports | $13/month | Subscribe at canva.com |

  **Total Day 1 subscriptions: $84/month**

- [ ] **Set up Notion workspace for project management**
  - Create a free Notion account at notion.so
  - Build the following workspace structure:
    - **Client Database:** Table with columns for Business Name, Contact Person, Email, Phone, Service Tier, Monthly Fee, Start Date, Contract End Date, Status
    - **Prospect Pipeline:** Kanban board with stages: Research, Outreach Sent, Follow-Up, Discovery Call Scheduled, Proposal Sent, Closed-Won, Closed-Lost
    - **Content Calendar:** Calendar view for tracking blog posts, social media content, and deliverables across all clients
    - **Task Manager:** Weekly task board for your personal workflow
    - **Templates Library:** Store all your templates (proposals, contracts, onboarding questionnaires, reports)
  - Spend 30-45 minutes building this foundation. It will save you hours every week going forward.

- [ ] **Create branded email (hello@yourdomain.com)**

  - Recommended addresses to create:

    - [yourname]@yourdomain.com (personal business email)
  - Configure your email signature with: Full name, title ("Founder & Digital Marketing Strategist"), phone number, website URL, and LinkedIn profile link

## Day 2: Online Presence (Tuesday)

### Morning Session (9:00 AM - 12:00 PM)

- [ ] **Register domain name ($12/year)**
  - Use Namecheap (namecheap.com), Google Domains, or Cloudflare Registrar
  - Recommended naming conventions:
    - [YourName]Marketing.com
    - [BrandName]Digital.com
    - [BrandName]SEO.com
  - Avoid hyphens, numbers, and overly long names
  - Purchase the .com extension. If budget permits, also secure .co and .net to protect your brand.
  - Enable WHOIS privacy protection (free on most registrars)
  - Connect the domain to your Google Workspace for email functionality

- [ ] **Set up website using Carrd.co ($19/year) or WordPress**
  - **Option A: Carrd.co ($19/year)** -- Best for launching quickly
    - Clean, single-page website that looks professional on day one
    - Sections to include: Hero (headline + CTA), Services Overview, About, Testimonials (use placeholder text like "Results coming soon" or a personal mission statement), Contact Form
    - Can be live within 2-3 hours
  - **Option B: WordPress with Starter Theme** -- Best for long-term SEO
    - Use a managed host like SiteGround ($3.99/month) or Bluehost
    - Install Astra or GeneratePress theme (free versions are excellent)
    - Essential plugins: Yoast SEO (free), WPForms Lite (contact form), UpdraftPlus (backups), WP Super Cache (speed)
    - Pages to create: Home, Services, About, Blog, Contact
    - Will take 4-6 hours to set up properly but provides a stronger SEO foundation
  - **Minimum website content needed today:**
    - **Headline:** "AI-Enhanced Digital Marketing for Local Businesses in Rockville & Montgomery County"
    - **Subheadline:** "Dominate local search, attract more customers, and grow your business with data-driven marketing strategies powered by cutting-edge AI technology."
    - **Services section:** Brief overview of your three tiers (Starter, Growth, Premium) with pricing and key features
    - **About section:** See the About Page copy provided later in this document
    - **Contact form:** Name, email, phone, business name, "How can we help?" message field
    - **Call-to-action:** "Schedule a Free 30-Minute Discovery Call" button linking to Calendly (set up a free Calendly account)

- [ ] **Create Google Business Profile**
  - Visit business.google.com and sign in with your Google Workspace account
  - Select "Service area business" (since you serve clients at their locations, not at a physical storefront)
  - Business category: "Marketing agency" (primary), "Internet marketing service" (secondary), "Search engine optimization expert" (additional)
  - Service area: Rockville, Bethesda, Silver Spring, Gaithersburg, Germantown, Potomac, Montgomery County
  - Add your business phone number, website URL, and hours of operation
  - Write a compelling business description (750 characters max):

    > "AI-enhanced digital marketing agency specializing in local SEO, content marketing, and Google Business Profile optimization for small businesses in Rockville and Montgomery County. We combine advanced AI technology with proven marketing strategies to help local businesses dominate Google search results, attract more customers, and grow revenue. Our transparent, results-driven approach delivers enterprise-grade marketing at small-business-friendly prices. Services include local SEO, content creation, social media management, PPC advertising, and review management."

  - Upload your logo and at least 3 photos (office/workspace, professional headshot, team photo or branded graphic)
  - Create your first GBP post: an "Offer" or "Update" announcing your launch

### Afternoon Session (1:00 PM - 4:00 PM)

- [ ] **Optimize LinkedIn profile**

  Your LinkedIn profile is your most important sales tool in the first 90 days. Treat it as a landing page, not a resume.

  - **Profile Photo:** Professional headshot with a clean background. If you do not have one, use an AI headshot generator (Headshot Pro, Aragon AI, or similar -- typically $29-49 one-time) or schedule a photo session this week.
  - **Banner Image:** Create a custom banner in Canva Pro (1584 x 396 pixels) featuring your business name, tagline, and the text "Helping Local Businesses Dominate Google Search."
  - **Headline** (see Social Media Profiles section below)
  - **About Section** (see Social Media Profiles section below)
  - **Featured Section:** Add 2-3 items:
    - Link to your website
    - A document or post about local SEO tips (create a simple PDF in Canva)
    - A link to schedule a discovery call (Calendly)
  - **Experience:** Add your new business with a detailed description of services
  - **Skills:** Add and get endorsements for: Local SEO, Digital Marketing, Content Marketing, Google Business Profile, Social Media Marketing, Search Engine Optimization, Google Ads, Small Business Marketing, AI in Marketing
  - **Recommendations:** Reach out to 3-5 professional contacts and ask for LinkedIn recommendations. Offer to write recommendations for them in return.

- [ ] **Create professional headshot**
  - Option 1: AI-generated headshot using Headshot Pro or Aragon AI ($29-49)
  - Option 2: Self-taken photo with a clean background, natural lighting, and professional attire. Use a portrait mode on your phone.

## Day 3: Service Preparation (Wednesday)

### Morning Session (9:00 AM - 12:00 PM)

- [ ] **Finalize service packages and pricing sheet**
  - Reference the Service_Packages.md document already created in this business plan
  - Create a clean, client-facing one-page pricing sheet in Canva Pro:
    - Three columns (Starter, Growth, Premium)
    - Key features listed with checkmarks

  - Create using Google Forms (free with Google Workspace) or Typeform (free tier)
  - Questions to include:
    1. Business name and legal entity name
    2. Primary contact name, email, and phone number
    3. Business address and service area
    4. Website URL (if applicable)
    5. Google Business Profile URL (if applicable)
    6. Social media profile links (all platforms)
    7. Top 3-5 services you offer
    8. Who is your ideal customer? (demographics, location, needs)
    9. Who are your top 3 local competitors?
    10. What are your top 3 business goals for the next 12 months?
    11. What marketing have you done in the past? What worked/did not work?
    12. Do you have brand guidelines (logo files, brand colors, fonts)?
    13. Do you have professional photos of your team, workspace, or work?
    14. How do you currently get most of your customers?
    15. What is your average customer lifetime value (approximate)?
    16. Login credentials for: Website CMS, Google Analytics, Google Search Console, Google Ads (share securely via LastPass, 1Password, or a secure link)
    17. Any specific keywords or search terms you want to rank for?
    18. Anything else we should know about your business?

  - Complete business verification with your EIN and business bank account
  - Set up your first invoice template
  - Alternatively, use Wave invoicing (already set up on Day 1) and add Stripe as a payment processor within Wave
  - Configure automatic payment reminders: 3 days before due date, on due date, 3 days after due date, 7 days after due date
  - Set up recurring invoices for monthly retainer clients

### Afternoon Session (1:00 PM - 4:00 PM)

- [ ] **Create proposal template**
  - Build a professional proposal template in Google Docs or Canva
  - Structure:
    1. **Cover Page:** Client business name, your business name and logo, date, "Digital Marketing Proposal"
    2. **Executive Summary:** 2-3 paragraphs summarizing the client's challenges and your proposed solution
    3. **Current State Analysis:** Summary of their current digital presence (website audit findings, GBP status, search visibility, social presence)
    4. **Recommended Strategy:** Specific service tier recommendation with justification
    5. **Scope of Work:** Detailed monthly deliverables
    6. **Timeline & Milestones:** Month-by-month expectations for the first 6 months
    7. **Investment:** Pricing, setup fee, payment terms
    8. **About Us:** Brief company overview and relevant experience
    9. **Next Steps:** How to move forward (sign agreement, complete onboarding questionnaire, schedule kickoff call)
    10. **Appendix:** Case studies or portfolio samples (add these as you complete your first projects)
  - Save as a template in your Notion Templates Library

- [ ] **Build SEO audit template**
  - Create a Google Doc or spreadsheet template for quick website audits
  - Sections to evaluate:
    - **Technical SEO:** Site speed (use Google PageSpeed Insights), mobile responsiveness, SSL certificate, sitemap, robots.txt, crawl errors
    - **On-Page SEO:** Title tags, meta descriptions, header tags (H1, H2, H3), keyword usage, image alt text, internal linking
    - **Local SEO:** Google Business Profile completeness, NAP consistency, local schema markup, citation presence, review profile
    - **Content:** Blog presence, content quality, content freshness, keyword targeting
    - **Competitive Position:** How they compare to top 3 local competitors for key search terms
  - Include a scoring system (1-10 for each category) and an overall grade (A through F)
  - This template will be your primary prospecting tool -- you will use it to audit potential clients' websites and present findings as a value-add in your outreach

- [ ] **Prepare case study template**
  - Structure:
    1. **Client Overview:** Industry, location, size (anonymized if needed)
    2. **Challenge:** What problems they were facing before working with you
    3. **Solution:** What services you provided and key strategies implemented
    4. **Results:** Specific metrics (traffic increase, ranking improvements, leads generated, revenue impact)
    5. **Timeline:** How long it took to achieve results
    6. **Client Testimonial:** Direct quote from the client
  - You will not have case studies yet, but having the template ready means you can document results from day one with your first client
  - **Tip:** During your first 2-3 months, consider offering a discounted "founding client" rate to 1-2 businesses in exchange for permission to use their results as a case study and testimonial

## Day 4: Prospecting (Thursday)

### Morning Session (9:00 AM - 12:00 PM)

- [ ] **Research 20 target businesses**
  - Use Google Maps, Yelp, and the Montgomery County Chamber of Commerce directory
  - Focus on these high-value industries:
    - Home services (plumbers, electricians, HVAC, landscapers, roofers, painters)
    - Healthcare practices (dentists, chiropractors, optometrists, veterinarians)
    - Professional services (law firms, accountants, financial advisors)
    - Personal services (salons, barbershops, fitness studios, spas)
    - Food and hospitality (restaurants, catering companies, bakeries)
  - For each business, record in your Notion Prospect Pipeline:
    - Business name and owner/manager name (find on LinkedIn, their website, or GBP)
    - Phone number and email address
    - Website URL
    - Google Business Profile URL
    - Number of Google reviews and average rating
    - Industry and estimated size (solo, 2-5 employees, 5-10, 10+)
    - Initial notes on their digital presence

- [ ] **Audit each business's website and Google presence**
  - Spend 10-15 minutes per business conducting a quick audit using your SEO audit template
  - Use these free tools:
    - Google PageSpeed Insights (pagespeed.web.dev) for site speed
    - Ubersuggest for keyword rankings and organic traffic estimates
    - Google search for "[business name] + [city]" to check their ranking
    - Google Business Profile listing review (completeness, photos, reviews, posts)
  - Document 3 specific, actionable findings for each business. These findings will form the basis of your personalized outreach.
  - Prioritize businesses that have:
    - Poor or nonexistent websites
    - Incomplete Google Business Profiles
    - Few or no Google reviews
    - No blog content
    - No social media presence
    - Competitors outranking them for obvious keywords

- [ ] **Customize email templates**
  - Use the personalized email drafts provided in the "5 Example Businesses to Contact" section below as models
  - Each outreach email must include:
    1. A personalized subject line mentioning their business name
    2. A specific observation about their digital presence (not generic flattery)
    3. One concrete, actionable finding from your audit
    4. A brief explanation of what you do and why it matters to them
    5. A clear, low-commitment call to action (free 15-minute call, not "buy my services")
  - Create 3 email variations to test which approach gets the best response rates:
    - **Template A: The Audit Approach** -- Lead with a specific finding from your website/GBP audit
    - **Template B: The Competitor Approach** -- Lead with how their competitor is outperforming them on Google
    - **Template C: The Value-First Approach** -- Lead with a free tip they can implement today, then offer to help with more

### Afternoon Session (1:00 PM - 4:00 PM)

- [ ] **Send first 10 outreach emails**
  - Send from your branded business email
  - Personalize each email with the specific findings from your morning audit
  - Send between 1:00 PM and 3:00 PM (Tuesday through Thursday afternoons have the highest open rates for B2B email)
  - Track each email in your Notion Prospect Pipeline (move card to "Outreach Sent" stage)
  - Use a mail merge tool like GMass or Mailmeteor if you want to send personalized emails at scale while tracking opens and clicks
  - **Do NOT send more than 15 emails from a new email account in a single day** -- this can trigger spam filters. Warm up your email account gradually.

- [ ] **Send 10 LinkedIn connection requests**
  - Find the owner or decision-maker for each of your target businesses on LinkedIn
  - Send a personalized connection request (300 character limit) with a brief note:

    > "Hi [Name], I noticed [Business Name] in [City] and was impressed by [specific positive observation]. I specialize in helping local businesses in Montgomery County improve their Google visibility. Would love to connect."

  - Do NOT pitch in the connection request. Wait until they accept, then send a follow-up message with value.
  - Also engage with their content if they post on LinkedIn (like, comment thoughtfully)

- [ ] **Set up follow-up reminders**
  - In your Notion Prospect Pipeline or Google Calendar, set reminders:
    - **Day 3 after initial email:** Follow-up email if no response
    - **Day 7 after initial email:** Second follow-up with additional value (share a relevant article or tip)
    - **Day 14 after initial email:** Final follow-up or phone call
  - Follow-up is where most sales happen. 80% of deals require 5+ touchpoints. Most people give up after 1-2.

## Day 5: Content & Follow-Up (Friday)

### Morning Session (9:00 AM - 12:00 PM)

- [ ] **Write and publish first LinkedIn post**
  - Post type: Educational/value-driven content
  - Topic suggestion: "5 Free Things Every Local Business in [City] Should Do Today to Get Found on Google"
  - Structure: Hook (first line grabs attention), 5 numbered tips with brief explanations, CTA (comment or DM for more details)
  - Length: 1,000-1,300 characters (LinkedIn's sweet spot for engagement)
  - Include 3-5 relevant hashtags: #LocalSEO #SmallBusinessMarketing #RockvilleBusiness #MontgomeryCounty #DigitalMarketing
  - Post between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM for maximum visibility
  - Engage with every comment within 1 hour of posting

- [ ] **Create first blog post for website**
  - Topic: "The Complete Guide to Google Business Profile Optimization for [City] Businesses"
  - Length: 1,500-2,000 words
  - Use Claude Pro to generate a comprehensive first draft, then edit thoroughly for:
    - Local relevance (mention specific Rockville/Montgomery County neighborhoods and landmarks)
    - Accuracy (verify all claims and recommendations)
    - Brand voice (professional but approachable)
    - SEO optimization (target keyword in title, H2s, first paragraph, and naturally throughout)
  - Include: actionable tips, screenshots or images, internal links (to your services page), and a CTA at the end
  - Publish on your website and share the link on LinkedIn and other social platforms

- [ ] **Follow up on any responses**
  - Check email for replies to yesterday's outreach
  - Respond to any inquiries within 1 hour
  - For interested prospects: Suggest a 15-30 minute discovery call and send your Calendly link
  - For "not right now" responses: Add to your nurture list and set a 30-day follow-up reminder
  - For questions about pricing: Send your one-page pricing sheet (created on Day 3) and offer to walk through it on a call

### Afternoon Session (1:00 PM - 4:00 PM)

- [ ] **Send second batch of 10 outreach emails**
  - Use the remaining 10 businesses from your Day 4 research
  - Apply what you learned from the first batch (subject line performance, any responses received)
  - Continue personalizing each email with specific audit findings
  - Track in Notion Prospect Pipeline

- [ ] **Plan Week 2 activities**
  - Review what worked and what did not work this week
  - Set specific goals for Week 2:
    - Number of outreach emails to send (target: 20)
    - Number of discovery calls to schedule (target: 2-3)
    - Content to publish (2 LinkedIn posts, 1 blog post)
    - Follow-up sequences to execute
  - Block time on your calendar for Week 2 activities
  - Identify any gaps in your process that need improvement

- [ ] **Review metrics and adjust strategy**
  - Email outreach: Open rate, response rate, positive response rate
  - LinkedIn: Connection acceptance rate, profile views, post engagement
  - Website: Visitors (check Google Analytics), contact form submissions
  - Document all metrics in a simple spreadsheet or Notion database for tracking over time

## Week 2 Preview

### Scaling Outreach to 20 Businesses

- Research and audit 20 additional businesses following the same process from Day 4
- Expand into industries you did not cover in Week 1
- Refine your outreach templates based on Week 1 response data (which subject lines got opened, which approaches generated replies)
- Target sending 4-5 outreach emails per day (Monday through Thursday) to stay within email warming limits
- Continue LinkedIn connection requests at a pace of 10-15 per week

### Follow-Up System

- Execute Day 3 follow-ups for all Week 1 outreach (Tuesday)
- Execute Day 7 follow-ups with added value for remaining non-responders (Friday)
- Implement a simple CRM follow-up cadence:
  - Touch 1: Initial outreach email (Week 1)
  - Touch 2: Follow-up email with additional insight, 3 days later
  - Touch 3: Value-add follow-up (share a relevant article or tip), 7 days after initial
  - Touch 4: Phone call or LinkedIn message, 10 days after initial
  - Touch 5: Final follow-up or breakup email, 14 days after initial
- Track all follow-up activity in Notion Prospect Pipeline

### First Discovery Call Preparation

- Prepare a structured discovery call script (30 minutes):
  - Minutes 1-5: Rapport building and agenda setting
  - Minutes 5-15: Ask about their business, goals, current marketing, biggest challenges
  - Minutes 15-22: Share your audit findings and specific recommendations
  - Minutes 22-27: Present recommended service tier and pricing
  - Minutes 27-30: Discuss next steps (proposal, start date, questions)
- Set up a Zoom or Google Meet link for virtual calls
- Prepare a screen-sharing walkthrough of their current Google presence vs. a competitor
- Have your pricing sheet, proposal template, and service agreement ready to send immediately after the call
- Practice the call script with a friend or family member at least twice before your first real discovery call

### Refining Pitch Based on Week 1 Feedback

- Analyze all responses (positive, negative, and non-responses) from Week 1
- Identify which industries showed the most interest
- Determine which outreach template (Audit, Competitor, or Value-First) performed best
- Adjust your target client profile if needed based on actual response patterns
- Update your email templates and pitch based on common questions or objections received
- Begin building a FAQ document based on real prospect questions to use in future conversations

### Additional Week 2 Goals

- Publish 2 LinkedIn posts (aim for Tuesday and Thursday mornings)
- Write and publish 1 additional blog post on your website
- Engage daily on LinkedIn: comment on 5-10 posts from local business owners and marketing thought leaders
- Join 2-3 local business groups on Facebook or LinkedIn (Montgomery County Chamber of Commerce, Rockville Business Network, local BNI chapters)
- Attend one virtual or in-person local business networking event if available
- Begin drafting your first email newsletter template for future use
- Review and refine your website based on any feedback or analytics data from Week 1
