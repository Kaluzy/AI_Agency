## 1. Cold Email Templates (Industry-Specific)

**General Guidelines for All Templates:**
- Personalize every email using merge tokens: {{FirstName}}, {{BusinessName}}, {{City}}
- Send from a professional domain email (e.g., outreach@youragency.com)
- Keep subject lines under 60 characters when possible
- Always include a single, clear call-to-action
- Reference a specific, real observation about their online presence when possible
- Never use spammy language ("guaranteed," "act now," "limited time")
- Aim for a conversational, peer-to-peer tone — not a sales pitch

## 3. Complete Outreach Sequence

This is the full multi-touch outreach sequence to be executed over 14 days for each prospect. Every touchpoint builds on the previous one and adds value. The goal is not to be pushy — it is to demonstrate competence and make it easy for the prospect to say yes.

---

### Day 1: Initial Email

Send the appropriate industry-specific cold email template from Section 1 above. Before sending, personalize the email by:

1. Visiting the prospect's website and Google Business Profile
2. Identifying at least one specific, real issue you can reference
3. Checking their review count and most recent review date
4. Noting their current Google ranking for their primary keyword + city
5. Replacing all merge tokens with accurate information

**Send time:** Tuesday through Thursday, between 7:00 AM and 9:00 AM local time. These windows consistently produce the highest open rates for B2B cold email.

**Tracking:** Log the send in your CRM/Lead_List. Set a follow-up reminder for Day 3.

## 5. Referral Program Design

Referrals are the lowest-cost, highest-quality source of new clients for any service business, including ours. This section outlines a structured referral program designed to turn satisfied clients and strategic partners into a consistent source of new business.

---

### Client Referral Program: "Refer & Save"

**Program Structure:**
- When an existing client refers a new business that signs a contract with us, the referring client receives **one month of service for free** (credited to their next invoice).
- There is no limit to the number of referrals a client can make. Each qualifying referral earns another free month.
- The referred business receives a **10% discount on their first three months** as a welcome incentive.

**Program Rules:**
- The referral must be a new business that is not already in our pipeline or CRM.
- The referred business must sign a contract and remain active for at least 30 days for the referral credit to apply.
- Credits are applied to the referring client's next monthly invoice and are not redeemable for cash.
- Referrals must be submitted through our referral form or by email introduction. We need to be able to track the source.

**How to Promote It:**
- Mention the referral program during every 90-day client review meeting.
- Include a referral program blurb in the footer of every monthly report email.
- Send a dedicated referral program email to all active clients quarterly.
- Create a simple one-page PDF explaining the program that clients can share.
